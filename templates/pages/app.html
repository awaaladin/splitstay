Android app

SplitPay on your phone

The same account, groups and payments as the website. Sign in with the details you already use.

Download for Android

Needs Android 8 or newer. iPhone isn't supported yet, so please use the website.

How to install it

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open. You can also find SplitPay.apk in the Files app, under Downloads.
  3. Tap Install, then open SplitPay and sign in.

If it doesn't download or install

SplitPay isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android and Chrome ask you to confirm. Find the message you see below.

Chrome says the file might be harmful

Chrome shows this for apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.

If you don't see that option, open Chrome's menu (the three dots), tap Downloads, and look for SplitPay.apk there.

Your phone says it can't install apps from this source

  1. Open your phone's Settings, then Apps.
  2. Tap Chrome. If you can't see it, tap See all apps first.
  3. Tap Install unknown apps and turn on Allow from this source.
  4. Go back to your downloads and tap SplitPay.apk again.

The wording changes a little between phone brands. If you can't find it, search for "unknown apps" in Settings. You can turn it off again after installing.

Google Play Protect shows a warning

Tap More details, then Install anyway.

The download doesn't start

Check that you have about 30 MB of free space and a working connection, then try again. If it still won't start, open Chrome's menu, tap the settings item, then Site settings, then Automatic downloads, and allow it.

It says the app wasn't installed when updating

Uninstall the old SplitPay app, then install the new one. Your groups and payments are safe because they're stored in your account. You'll just need to sign in again.
