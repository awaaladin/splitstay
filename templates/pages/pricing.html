Fees

Free until the money goes out. Then one line.

Creating a group, inviting people and paying your share costs nothing with us. When a group is paid out, SplitStay takes one fee from the pooled money. It is shown in the group summary before you start, and as its own entry on the ledger and the receipt afterwards.

Covering a bill exactly? The fee comes out of the pool, so set the target a little above the bill. The group form shows the fee and the net amount as you type.

No hidden deductions

The fee is recorded as a separate entry, never subtracted silently from someone's share.

No fee on failure

If a payment to a bill or bank fails, your money stays in the pot and the admin can retry. The fee is part of the payout record, not a charge on top.

No lending, no interest

We never advance money on anyone's behalf, so there is nothing to charge interest on.
