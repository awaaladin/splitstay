About SplitStay

Built on an old idea: money is easier when everyone can see it.

In an ajo or esusu circle, trust doesn't come from a company. It comes from the fact that everyone can see who has paid. SplitStay brings that to rent, light bills and family upkeep, and adds the part that is hardest to do by hand: paying the bill directly.

The problem

Someone always ends up being the bank.

Four people share a flat. One person pays the landlord and then spends two weeks asking for their money back. Another pays the light bill "for now" and quietly absorbs a loss. Families do the same with upkeep: the one who earns most becomes the treasurer, and the spreadsheet lives in their head.

It is rarely about the money itself. It is about the awkwardness: chasing friends, doubting who paid, and being unable to prove it either way.

What we believe

{% icon "users" 20 %}

Everyone sees everything

The ledger is open to every member, not just the admin. Who paid, when, how much, and what was paid out. Nothing is hidden and nothing is edited after the fact.

{% icon "receipt" 20 %}

Fees you can point to

Our fee is one line, recorded like any other entry, and shown before and after the payout. No silent deductions, no percentage buried in an exchange rate.

{% icon "shield-check" 20 %}

Never a lender

We never pay a bill and wait to be reimbursed. Money moves out only once your group has put it in, so nobody ends up in debt to the platform.

How we handle your money

Plainly, step by step.

  1. 01

    Collection. Each person pays their own share through Paystack, using a card, bank transfer or USSD. We never see your card details.

  2. 02

    Counting. A group's total is always added up from confirmed payments, never kept as a separate number that can drift. A payment is counted once, even if the payment company tells us twice.

  3. 03

    Payout. When the target is met, the bill is paid or the transfer is sent automatically, to the destination the group was created with. If an attempt is uncertain, we check with the provider rather than risk paying twice.

  4. 04

    Protection. Bank account numbers are encrypted at rest and only the last four digits are ever displayed.

Straight about the limits

What it doesn't do yet.

  • {% icon "x" 18 "mt-1 flex-none" %}No refunds yet. That is why a group that has received money is paid out rather than cancelled, and why members and shares lock after the first payment.
  • {% icon "x" 18 "mt-1 flex-none" %}Electricity bills only, for now. Rent, family and custom groups pay by bank transfer. Airtime, cable and water are planned.
  • {% icon "x" 18 "mt-1 flex-none" %}Members need an account first. You can invite only people who have already registered.
