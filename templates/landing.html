Group contributions, done properly

Shared costs, settled in the open.

Roommates, families and friends pool money toward rent, light bills, subscriptions or family upkeep. When the target is met, SplitStay pays the bill or the recipient directly, and everyone can see exactly who paid and what was paid out.

How the money moves

  1. {% icon "users" 18 %}

    Everyone pays their own share

    Equal by default, or set exact amounts for uneven splits.

  2. {% icon "wallet" 18 %}

    The group fills toward its target

    Watch it fill live. Nothing is paid out early unless the admin allows it.

  3. {% icon "zap" 18 %}

    The bill or landlord is paid directly

    Electricity tokens are bought for your meter; rent goes to the recipient's bank account.

  4. {% icon "receipt" 18 %}

    Everything lands in the ledger

    Contributions, the service fee and the payout, visible to every member.

Open to every member

The way an ajo circle works: everyone can see who has paid and who hasn't. The ledger is not an admin-only view.

One explicit fee

A flat or percentage service fee is taken at payout and recorded as its own line, never as a silent deduction.

Recurring, without the admin work

A monthly light-bill group reopens on the same day with the same members and split.
