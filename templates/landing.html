    {# ───────────────────────────── 3. STORY ───────────────────────────── #}

    A month, three ways it usually goes wrong

    The money was never the hard part. The chasing was.

    01 · The rent

    One landlord. Four people. One clean transfer.

    Everyone pays their own share into the pot, equal or not. When it's full, the landlord gets a single transfer to the account on file, with a receipt to match. No screenshots of bank alerts in the group chat.

    02 · The light

    The meter doesn't wait for whoever "will sort it".

    Put in the meter number once. When the pool reaches the target, SplitStay buys the units from your disco and the token lands in everyone's ledger. Whoever is home gets light; nobody is out of pocket.

    03 · The upkeep

    Uneven shares, the same day every month.

    Families split by what each person can carry, not by headcount. Set the amounts once. When a cycle is paid out, the next one opens itself with the same people and the same split, and the right ones get a nudge as the day nears.
