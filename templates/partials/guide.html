The complete guide

Never used it before? Start here and follow along.

This guide takes you from nothing to a paid bill, one small step at a time. No jargon, and no step you can't undo before money is involved. A good example to keep in mind: four students in an off-campus flat who need to pay the rent, the light and the Wi-Fi without anyone being the "bank".

{# ───── 1. GET STARTED ───── #}

1 Get started

  1. Create your account

    Press Create account at the top right. Fill in your full name, your email address and a password of at least 8 characters. The phone number is optional, but add it: friends can then invite you with your number, and you can sign in with it too.

    {% icon "info" 16 %} Tip. Use an email you check often. Group invitations and payment confirmations show up in the app, and your email is your sign-in name.

  2. Sign in later with email or phone

    On the Sign in page, type your email or your phone number in the first box and your password in the second. Forgot which one you used? Try both.

  3. Save your bank account only if you'll receive money

    You only need this when a group will pay out to you, for example if you collect rent from housemates and then pay the landlord yourself. Open your name at the top right, choose Profile & bank, pick your bank, type your 10-digit account number and the name on the account, then press Save account.

    {% icon "lock" 16 %} Your account number is encrypted. The app only ever shows the last four digits.

{# ───── 2. START A GROUP ───── #}

2 Start a group for the person organising

The person who creates the group becomes its admin. Do this once; everyone else just pays.

  1. Press New group

    You'll find it on your dashboard. Then pick what the money is for: Rent, Electricity (NEPA), Subscription, Family upkeep or Custom. It only changes the icon and some sensible defaults.

  2. Fill in the details

    • Group name. Something your friends will recognise, like "Flat 4B light bill".
    • Target amount. The total everyone must raise together, in naira. Example: 30,000.
    • Due date. When the money is needed. People are reminded as it nears.

    {% icon "info" 16 %} Fee tip. A small service fee (₦150 unless set otherwise) is taken from the pool when it's paid out, and the summary on the right shows it. If a bill must be covered exactly, set the target a little higher.

  3. Choose if it repeats optional

    Tick Repeat this group and choose monthly, quarterly or yearly. When one round is paid out, the next opens by itself with the same people and the same split, so you never recreate it.

  4. Say where the money goes

    Pick one:

    • Bank transfer. Choose the bank, type the 10-digit account number and the account name. Use this for rent, or money for a family member.
    • Pay an electricity bill. Choose your electricity company, whether the meter is Prepaid or Postpaid, and type the meter number. Press Check this meter: if it's correct you'll see the customer's name in green before you go on.

  5. Decide about early payout

    The box Allow me to pay out before the target is reached lets you (only you) send what's been collected so far, for when the bill is due before everyone has paid. Leave it off if you'd rather wait for the full amount.

  6. Add the people

    Under Who's in? type each person's email or phone number. They must already have a SplitStay account, so ask them to register first. Leave Equal share empty to split fairly, or type an exact amount for someone who should pay more or less. Watch the Summary on the right: it shows what each person owes as you type.

    Finally press Create group. Everyone you added gets an invitation.

{# ───── 3. JOIN ───── #}

3 Join a group when you've been invited

  1. Find your invitation

    Sign in. At the top of your dashboard there's an Invitations box, and a number appears on the bell. Each invitation shows who invited you, your share and the due date.

  2. Press Join (or Decline)

    You can't pay or see who has paid until you join, which keeps things private until you agree. If the share looks wrong, decline and ask the organiser to fix it, or join and ask them to change it before anyone pays.

    {% icon "info" 16 %} You can leave a group any time before you've paid. After that, your payment is part of the group's money.

{# ───── 4. PAY ───── #}

4 Pay your share

  1. Open the group and find Your part

    It shows your share, what you've paid and what's Outstanding. You can only pay your own share. Nobody can pay for you, and you can't overpay.

  2. Press Pay ₦…

    You're taken to the secure payment page of the payment company (Paystack). Pay with your card, a bank transfer or a USSD code, whichever you prefer. Don't close the page until it sends you back.

  3. Wait for the green tick

    You come back to SplitStay and see a tick draw itself with Payment confirmed, the amount, a reference number and the time. Keep the reference if you ever need to ask about it.

    • "Confirming your payment…" means the bank is still answering. Leave the page open or come back later; it completes by itself and you'll get a notification.
    • "Payment didn't go through" means nothing was taken, and you can press Pay again.

    {% icon "shield-check" 16 %} If you press Pay twice by accident, you won't be charged twice for the same payment.

{# ───── 5. WATCH ───── #}

5 Watch it fill

  1. Read the group page

    • The pot rises as money comes in, with the amount collected, what's still needed and how many people have paid.
    • Who has paid lists every member as Paid, Part-paid or Not yet paid. Everyone in the group can see this. That openness is the point.
    • Ledger is the record of every payment, fee and payout, newest first. Press Full history for all of it.

    The page refreshes itself every few seconds, so you don't need to reload it.

  2. Use the bell and the Activity page

    The bell (top right) tells you when someone pays, when the target is reached, when the money is paid out, and when a payment is due soon. Activity in the menu shows them all. Click one to jump to the group.

{# ───── 6. PAYOUT ───── #}

6 The payout and the receipt

  1. It happens by itself

    The moment the pot reaches the target, the group is marked Funded and the payment starts. For a bank transfer, the money is sent to the account you set. For an electricity bill, SplitStay buys the units for your meter. Nobody has to press anything.

  2. Open the receipt

    When it completes, Your part shows View receipt. The receipt lists who paid, the total, the service fee on its own line, and the amount actually paid. For electricity it also shows your token: the number you type into your meter. Press Copy token to copy it, or Print or save as PDF to keep it.

  3. If a payment fails

    Sometimes a bank or electricity company says no (for example, a wrong meter number). Your money is safe in the pot. The admin sees Retry payment; after fixing the problem with Edit, pressing it tries again with a fresh attempt. It never pays twice.

  4. Paying out early admin, if you allowed it

    If the bill is due before everyone has paid, press Pay out ₦… now and confirm. The group closes and the service fee is taken from what's collected. Late payers can't add more after that.

{# ───── 7. MANAGE ───── #}

7 Managing a group admin

  1. Add, remove or re-split

    Use Add member, the pencil beside a name to set an exact share (clear it to go back to equal), or the × to remove someone. Everyone else's share rebalances so the total always equals the target.

    {% icon "lock" 16 %} Why can't I change things? Once the first payment arrives, members, shares and the target are locked. Otherwise someone could end up having paid more or less than they owe.

  2. Edit or cancel

    Edit changes the name, due date and destination. Cancel group is only available while nobody has paid, because there are no refunds in this version.

  3. Repeating groups

    A repeating group opens its next round on its own after payout or the due date. The new round keeps the same people and shares, and everyone gets a notification. The date stays on the same day of the month.

{# ───── 8. WORDS ───── #}

8 Words explained

Pot
All the money collected so far for one group.
Target
The amount the pot must reach before it's paid out.
Share
Your part of the target. Equal unless the admin set it differently.
Admin
The person who created the group and manages it.
Ledger
The permanent list of every payment, fee and payout in a group.
Payout
The moment the pot is sent on: to a bank account or to a bill.
Service fee
A small, flat charge for SplitStay, shown as its own line.
Token
The number for a prepaid meter. Typing it in loads the units.
Cycle
One round of a repeating group, such as one month's rent.
Reference
A unique code for a payment, useful if you ever need to ask about one.
{# ───── 9. FAQ ───── #}

9 Questions people ask

Is my money safe?{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

Every payment is recorded before anything else happens, and a payment is only counted once, even if the payment company tells us twice. Money moves only to the account or meter the group was created with, and the admin cannot change the destination after the first payment.

Someone hasn't paid. What now?{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

The group stays open past its due date (it shows as Overdue) and still accepts payments. Late payers are reminded. If the bill can't wait, the admin can pay out early, if that was allowed when the group was created.

I paid but it says Pending.{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

Banks sometimes take a minute. Leave the confirmation page open; it checks every few seconds. If it stays pending for a long time, tell the admin your payment reference, which you can find in the confirmation page.

Can I get a refund?{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

Not in this version. That's why a group that has received money can't simply be cancelled; it's paid out instead. Check the amount and destination before you pay.

Does SplitStay lend money?{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

Never. Nothing is paid on your behalf in advance. Bills and transfers are paid only from money your group has already put in.

What does it cost?{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

Creating and joining groups is free. A small service fee is taken from the pot when it's paid out, shown beforehand in the group summary and afterwards as its own line on the ledger and receipt.

Can I use my phone?{% icon "chevron-down" 18 %}

Yes. The site works in any phone browser, and there's a light and a dark mode (the sun or moon button at the top right).
